# Privacy Policy for Brownian NTA Lab

**Effective Date:** August 12, 2026  
**App Name:** Brownian NTA Lab  
**Developer:** thwump / Open Scientific Tools  

---

### 1. Overview
**Brownian NTA Lab** is an open-source educational and scientific mobile application designed to perform **Nanoparticle Tracking Analysis (NTA)** and Brownian motion physics calculations directly on your Android device.

We respect your privacy. **Brownian NTA Lab does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data, location data, analytics, or user identifiers.** All camera processing, video file analysis, and trajectory physics calculations are performed 100% locally on your device.

---

### 2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

#### 📷 **Camera Access (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**
- **Purpose:** Used exclusively to display a live camera preview and extract real-time image frames for microscopic particle tracking when using clip-on microscope lenses.
- **Data Handling:** Camera frames are analyzed in-memory (RAM) solely during active tracking sessions. No photos, camera frames, or video feeds are ever recorded, stored on disk, or transmitted over the internet or any network.

#### 📁 **File & Storage Access (Local Video Loading & Data Export)**
- **Video Selection:** The app utilizes the standard Android system file picker (`ACTION_GET_CONTENT`) to allow you to select previously recorded video files from your device for offline analysis. Video files are processed strictly locally on your phone.
- **Data Export:** The app exports trajectory datasets, Mean Square Displacement (MSD) curves, and extracted physical constants into standard `.json` and `.csv` files saved directly to your device's local storage (e.g., Downloads or Documents folder) or shared via system share sheets.
- **Data Handling:** No files on your device are read or accessed without your explicit interaction through the Android system file picker.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Network Access
- **No Internet Required:** Brownian NTA Lab functions completely offline. The app contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, tracking scripts, or remote server connections.
- **External Web Links:** The app includes optional links to external educational resources (such as Wikipedia articles on Jean Perrin, Albert Einstein, and fundamental constants). Tapping these links opens your device's default web browser.

---

### 4. Data Security & Storage
All data generated by the app (including particle trajectories, temperature/viscosity parameters, and physical constants) remains strictly on your local device. You retain full ownership and control over all exported data files.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Because Brownian NTA Lab does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The app is safe for use by students and researchers of all ages.
